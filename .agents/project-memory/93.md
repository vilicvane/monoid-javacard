## PUT KEY length restriction

The same 272B P-256 public-key/full-parameter request from record92 was reframed
into protected-data fragments of128/128/24B, retaining the full-command MAC.
The first128B returned9000; the second128B returned6A86 while it was still a

This strengthens the cumulative-length explanation: the rejection occurs at
256B accumulated data, before final-command completion/MAC verification. In
record92 a first255B fragment was accepted, short RSA chaining succeeded, and
both long RSA and ECC commands failed. These observations point to a PUT KEY

case or a universal maximum for other GP commands. Sending smaller fragments
does not resolve it. GP2.3 section11.1.5.1 documents6A86 for unsupported chaining
or an exceeded implementation chain limit.

The last two rows deliberately use nonstandard diagnostic encodings. They are
not recommended deployment formats. Together they isolate rejection of the
trailing fields: accepting FF-prefixed components does not establish complete
Format2 support on this firmware. An observable key entry remains after a
failed command; do not equate6A80 with no state change. The failed-format domains
remained SELECTABLE rather than becoming PERSONALIZED.

The table entry is `C0 06 01 73 A1 80 A0 03`. Its presence alone does not prove
the public components are complete or usable; these errored imports were not
used for verification. GP2.3 section11.8.2.3.3 requires atomic processing of a
multi-component key. We have established this observable compatibility/rollback
anomaly, not its internal implementation cause. The proposed ECC Format2 path
was therefore not attempted. Deleting each whole temporary SD succeeded.
